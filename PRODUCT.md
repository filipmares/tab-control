# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing many Chrome tabs who need to reduce clutter and reorganize
their active browsing context quickly, without losing confidence in what the
extension will change.

## Product Purpose

Tab Control provides focused, local tab-management actions for closing
duplicates, sorting and grouping tabs, gathering tabs into one window, and
restoring recently closed items. Success means users can understand the
available operations at a glance, act quickly, and trust the outcome.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, dependable. The Braun influence should come through
clarity, proportion, legible controls, and disciplined use of color rather
than decorative nostalgia.

## Anti-references

Avoid retro cosplay, ornamental knobs, skeuomorphic control panels, generic
SaaS cards, and visual details that imply function without serving it.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action immediately legible.
- Communicate consequence before decoration.
- Use restraint to increase confidence, not to suppress hierarchy.
- Let every visual distinction encode meaning.
- Keep feedback close to the action that caused it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, conspicuous focus
states, readable text at the popup's fixed size, non-color status cues, and
reduced-motion preferences.
